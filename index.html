About

Behind the shutter

I'm Prabhav, a Melbourne-based photographer, I specialise in capturing moments that matter, from events and birthdays to portraits, headshots, product photography, and fast-paced sports action. Whether it’s a personal memory or a professional shoot, I focus on clean, high-quality visuals that tell a story and stand out. Now open for bookings. I shoot on a Sony A7 III, and truly believe in providing authentic, high-quality images that capture the essence of every moment.

What started at drift days and car meets has grown into portrait and family sessions, and I'm now taking on a limited number of client bookings. Every shoot gets the same treatment: careful framing on location, then a considered edit pass rather than a preset dumped on top.
